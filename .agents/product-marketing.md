## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Axcell runs your finance function so you get reliable numbers before every decision, not after it.
**What it does:** Managed finance (bookkeeping, accounting, monthly reporting), fractional CFO, internal audit and controls, ERP selection and implementation (vendor-independent; Odoo and ERPNext practices), business valuation, Islamic finance advisory, investor readiness and due-diligence support, and training (Financial Leadership Workshop with CCIFE).
**Product category:** Managed finance services / fractional CFO.
**Business model:** Monthly retainers (operations), annual programmes (internal audit), project and retainer mix (advisory, ERP, valuation), workshops (training).
**Brand name:** Axcell / «أكسل». Use "Axcell Financial Advisory" / «أكسل للاستشارات المالية». Never "Axcell Business Services".
**Founder:** Hazem Kassem, 25+ years of CFO experience.
**Website:** axcellservice.com · info@axcellservice.com

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** SMEs and family businesses in Egypt and Saudi Arabia, typically without a full-time CFO.
**Decision-makers:** Owners, CEOs, general managers; secondary: investors and their investees.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get the few numbers that drive decisions, on time
- Get a straight answer from finance the same day ("if I discount 5%, do I make or lose money?")
- Stop depending on one employee who holds all the finance knowledge
- Be ready for investors, banks, auditors and tax authorities (ETA, ZATCA)

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Finance in most SMEs records the past; it doesn't support decisions.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- A single in-house accountant: key-person risk, no review, no CFO judgement
- Audit firms: once a year, backward-looking
- ERP vendors: sell software, not decisions
**What it costs them:** Late or wrong decisions (discounts, purchasing, hiring), cash squeezes in growth, compliance penalties, lower valuation.
**Emotional tension:** Owners feel they "should know" their numbers and don't.

## Differentiation
- Senior CFO judgement + a supervised team + documented processes ("reliable numbers are a system, not a person" «الأرقام الموثوقة نظام… لا شخص»)
- Vendor-independent ERP advice, by accountants, not resellers
- Arabic-first, MENA-native (ETA, ZATCA, EAS, IFRS, zakat)

## Brand Voice
- Direct, provocative, data-backed; challenges beliefs, never mocks people
- Every claim sourced or labelled as an example with stated assumptions
- No invented stories, clients, statistics or testimonials
- CTA: "message me" / «راسلني» only. Never "comment a word".
- Colours: navy #0A2A43, dark grey #2C2C2C, white, one red accent #C8102E

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "My accountant handles it" | Your accountant records; who reviews, and who tells you the number before you decide? |
| "Outsourcing means losing control" | You keep ownership of systems and data; we work inside your system under your oversight |
| "It's expensive" | Don't argue on price; show the cost of one wrong decision or one late report |

**Anti-persona:** Companies wanting the cheapest bookkeeping only, with no interest in reporting or decisions.
